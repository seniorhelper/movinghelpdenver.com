How Moving Help Denver works

  1. Describe the job

    Pick load, unload or both, the hours you guess, the crew size, the date and the stairs. Add name and phone.

  2. We read it

    A person checks that it is a real job, not spam.

  3. Up to three crews get it

    Each has an active Colorado PUC household-goods permit, insurance in force and a BBB A+, checked by us before it receives requests.

  4. You compare hourly quotes

    Ask each what the minimum is, when the clock starts, and what the rate includes. Take one, or none.

Cost

Nothing to you, and no obligation.

What to have ready when the phone rings

  • The address at both ends and where the truck will park.
  • A short list of the heavy stuff: appliances, safes, sectionals.
  • Whether the elevator needs booking or the building wants an insurance certificate.
  • The truck pickup time, if you are renting one.

Questions to put to each crew

  1. What is the hourly rate for my crew size, and what is the minimum?
  2. Do you bring pads, straps, a dolly and a ramp, or should I?
  3. Does the clock start when you arrive or when you start lifting?
  4. What happens if the job runs past the booked hours?
  5. How do I file a complaint if something is damaged?

If a quote looks too cheap

Ask what is missing. A low hourly rate with a long minimum, or a crew that does not carry any equipment, can cost more than a higher rate that includes everything.
