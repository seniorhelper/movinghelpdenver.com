Privacy Policy

Last changed November 11, 2026. This site belongs to Eye To Ad Media of Denver, Colorado.

What we get

Your answers on the job form: job type, hours, crew size, ZIP, date, stairs, any note, name, phone and optional email. We save the time of submission, the terms version and the exact consent text shown, as a record of your choice.

What happens to it

  • A person looks it over.
  • It goes to up to three labor crews.
  • We may call, text or email you about it.
  • We keep records to deter spam and answer legal questions.

No data brokers. No advertising trackers on this site.

Crews

Separate companies with their own privacy practices. We ask them to use your details only for your job.

Calls and texts

Only if you ticked the box. Autodialed calls, texts and recorded messages are possible. Text STOP to stop texts.

How long

Up to 24 months, then removed. Ask for sooner removal anytime.

Access and deletion

Email zach@eyetoad.com. We answer requests from anyone, wherever they live.

Safety

HTTPS on the form; no personal data in web addresses.

Records of consent

We store what the consent box said when you ticked it, together with the time and version of the terms. It is our proof if someone later asks what you agreed to.

Third parties on this site

There are none. Pages use only files served from this site, and the form sends directly to our lead server.

Outside links

Where we cite city, state or business pages, those sites follow their own privacy rules.

Age

For adults 18 and over.
