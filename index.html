Quick answers

Is Moving Help Denver a moving company?

No. It is a free referral service run by Eye To Ad Media in Denver. We pass your job to up to three crews we checked, and you deal with the crew directly.

How much does moving labor cost in Denver?

Published figures vary. One Denver mover's price guide lists 85 to 120 dollars an hour per person for loading or unloading a rental truck. U-Haul's Denver labor listings show hourly rates from about 59 to 250 dollars, mostly around 100 to 150, usually for a two-person crew with a minimum of two hours.

Do I need a permit to park a rental truck in Denver?

Only if the truck will sit on a metered space or otherwise occupy the street, sidewalk or alley. Denver's transportation department handles street-occupancy permits and meter bagging, but its page does not name moving trucks, so call before moving day.

Are the crews you send licensed?

We only send jobs to companies that hold an active Colorado PUC household-goods permit, carry insurance, and have a Better Business Bureau A+ rating. We check each one before it receives a request.
